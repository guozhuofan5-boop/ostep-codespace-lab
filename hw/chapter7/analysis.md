## Q4
- Prediction / 预测:When all jobs arrive at the same time and are already listed from shortest to longest, SJF should have the same turnaround time as FIFO. If a longer job appears first, however, SJF should perform better./ 当所有作业同时到达，并且已经按照从短到长的顺序排列时，预计SJF和FIFO的周转时间相同。但如果较长的作业排在前面，SJF应该表现得更好。
- Reasoning / 理由: FIFO follows the original submission order, while SJF selects the shortest available job first. Therefore, when the submission order is already shortest-first, both algorithms use the same schedule. If a long job is placed first, FIFO forces the shorter jobs to wait behind it, creating the convoy effect. SJF avoids this by running the shorter jobs first./ FIFO按照原来的提交顺序执行，而SJF优先选择最短的作业。因此，当提交顺序已经是从短到长时，两种算法的执行顺序相同。如果长作业排在最前面，FIFO会让后面的短作业一直等待，从而产生护航效应；SJF则会先执行短作业，因此可以避免这种情况。
- Verified result / 验证结果:For jobs with lengths 300, 200, and 100, FIFO produced an average turnaround time of 466.67, while SJF produced 333.33. When the jobs were already sorted as 100, 200, and 300, both algorithms produced 333.33. / 对于长度为300、200、100的作业，FIFO的平均周转时间为466.67，而SJF为333.33。当作业按照100、200、300的顺序排列时，两种算法的平均周转时间均为333.33。
- Analysis / 分析:The experiment supports the prediction. The main difference appears when a long job is placed before shorter ones. FIFO keeps the shorter jobs waiting, whereas SJF rearranges them to reduce their waiting and turnaround times. / 实验结果支持之前的预测。两种算法的主要区别出现在长作业排在短作业之前的情况下。FIFO会让短作业等待，而SJF会重新安排执行顺序，从而减少短作业的等待时间和周转时间。

## Q5
- Prediction / 预测: SJF and RR should have the same response time when the jobs are submitted from shortest to longest and the quantum is large enough that the earlier jobs can finish without being interrupted.  / 当作业按照从短到长的顺序提交，并且时间片足够大，使前面的作业能够在一次时间片内完成时，预计SJF和RR的响应时间相同。
- Reasoning / 理由:Under SJF, a job starts after all shorter jobs before it have finished. Under RR, the start time depends on how much CPU time the previous jobs receive during their turns. If every earlier job is shorter than or equal to the quantum, RR allows each of them to finish before moving on. Therefore, the response times can be the same as SJF./ 在SJF中，一个作业需要等前面更短的作业完成后才能开始。在RR中，作业的开始时间取决于前面的作业在各自时间片中获得多少CPU时间。如果前面的每个作业都不超过时间片长度，那么RR可以让它们在下一轮之前完成，因此响应时间就可能与SJF相同。
- Verified result / 验证结果:For jobs of 100, 200, and 300, RR with a quantum of 200 produced an average response time of 133.33, which is the same as SJF. With a quantum of 100, the average response time decreased to 100.00./ 对于长度为100、200和300的作业，RR的时间片为200时，平均响应时间为133.33，与SJF相同。当时间片减少到100时，平均响应时间下降到100.00。
- Analysis / 分析: The results confirm the prediction. With a quantum of 200, the first two jobs can complete without being interrupted, so RR behaves similarly to SJF with respect to response time. With a smaller quantum of 100, the second job is interrupted, allowing the third job to receive CPU time earlier. / 实验结果验证了预测。当时间片为200时，前两个作业可以在被抢占之前完成，因此RR在响应时间方面与SJF表现相同。当时间片降低到100时，第二个作业会被中断，使第三个作业能够更早获得CPU。

## Q6
- Prediction / 预测:The average response time of SJF should increase linearly as the lengths of the jobs increase. For job sets 10,20,30; 100,200,300; and 1000,2000,3000, the expected averages are 13.33, 133.33, and 1333.33./ 预计随着作业长度增加，SJF的平均响应时间也会呈线性增长。对于10、20、30；100、200、300；以及1000、2000、3000这三组作业，预计平均响应时间分别为13.33、133.33和1333.33。
- Reasoning / 理由:SJF is non-preemptive, meaning that once a job starts, it must finish before another job can run. Therefore, each job's response time depends on the total length of the jobs scheduled before it. Increasing all job lengths by the same factor increases the response times by that same factor./ SJF属于非抢占式调度，这意味着一个作业开始后必须运行结束，其他作业才能执行。因此，每个作业的响应时间取决于它之前作业的总长度。如果所有作业长度同时按照相同比例增加，响应时间也会按照相同比例增加。
- Verified result / 验证结果:The measured average response times were 13.33, 133.33, and 1333.33 for the three groups of jobs, respectively.
  / 实验得到的三组平均响应时间分别为13.33、133.33和1333.33。
- Analysis / 分析:The results show a clear linear relationship. Increasing the job lengths by a factor of 10 also increased the average response time by a factor of 10. This demonstrates that SJF can have poor response time when the jobs become large./ 实验结果显示出了明显的线性关系。作业长度增加10倍后，平均响应时间也增加了10倍。这说明当作业规模较大时，SJF的响应时间可能会变得较差。

## Q7
- Prediction / 预测: As the RR time quantum becomes larger, the average response time should also increase. Once the quantum reaches the job length, RR effectively becomes similar to FIFO. For three jobs of length 100, the expected average response times for quantum values of 1, 10, 50, and 100 are 1.00, 10.00, 50.00, and 100.00. / 随着RR时间片变大，预计平均响应时间也会增加。当时间片达到作业长度时，RR的行为会逐渐接近FIFO。对于三个长度均为100的作业，当时间片分别为1、10、50和100时，预计平均响应时间分别为1.00、10.00、50.00和100.00。
- Reasoning / 理由: In RR, each job waits for the jobs before it to receive their turns before getting its first CPU time. For three equal-length jobs, the response times are 0, q, and 2q, so the average is q. Therefore, increasing the quantum directly increases the average response time./ 在RR中，每个作业都需要等待前面的作业完成各自的时间片后，才能第一次获得CPU。对于三个长度相同的作业，它们的响应时间分别为0、q和2q，因此平均值就是q。所以，时间片越大，平均响应时间也会直接增加。
- Verified result / 验证结果: The experiment gave average response times of 1.00, 10.00, 50.00, and 100.00 for quantum values of 1, 10, 50, and 100. The corresponding average turnaround times were 299, 290, 250, and 200. / 实验结果显示，当时间片为1、10、50和100时，平均响应时间分别为1.00、10.00、50.00和100.00。对应的平均周转时间分别为299、290、250和200。
- Analysis / 分析: The experimental results matched the prediction. A smaller quantum improves responsiveness because every job gets a chance to run sooner, but it also causes more interruptions and increases turnaround time. When the quantum reaches 100, each job can finish in a single turn, making RR effectively equivalent to FIFO./ 实验结果与预测一致。较小的时间片能够提高响应速度，因为每个作业都可以更早获得CPU，但同时也会增加中断次数，从而影响周转时间。当时间片达到100时，每个作业都可以在一次执行中完成，因此RR实际上与FIFO非常接近。
